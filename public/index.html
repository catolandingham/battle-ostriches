How to play

Ostrich

  • Keep one of your eggs alive for rounds. Both birds always see each other; your eggs stay hidden.
  • Walk up to squares. You may pass over anything, but not land on a wrecked square or on an animal you can see. Carry an egg square, never onto or next to a leaf.
  • Sit on an egg as long as you like. A sitting ostrich is seen by anyone who can see it.
  • Kick an animal next to you instead of walking. Not while carrying, and not a buried badger. A vulture is pushed straight away squares, around the edge, and stops before a wreck or another animal. A hunter lands squares away (a badger ) on a random square, not on an animal or a wreck, and not close enough to catch you. An egg there does not break.
  • Stand or sit on each egg within rounds or it goes cold. A carried egg is not warmed. The ring on an egg counts down.
  • Land on a leaf, or stay on one, every turns or you starve.
  • A stone thrown at your square is kicked back at the vulture. Squares next to you are not safe.

Vulture

  • Break every egg. Cold eggs and a starved ostrich count too.
  • Fly up to squares, or Throw at your square or one next to it. A stone wrecks the square it lands on, except a leaf or a rock pile. A hunter there lands squares away on a random square, not on an animal or a wreck, and not close enough to catch you when such a square exists. An egg on that square does not break. A buried badger still comes up. Never at your teammate's square.
  • Each landing sniffs: Egg nearby! or No egg nearby (within square). The result stays until you fly again.
  • You hold stone. Land on rocks to get another. Landing on an egg finds it.

3 players

  • Hunter goal:
  • Turn order: vulture, ostrich, hunter. Each turn has a 10-second shot clock; if it runs out the turn ends.
  • The hunter (hyena or badger) walks up to squares. When a bird is within squares, an outline shows where it may be. Birds see the hunter within squares, never a buried badger.
  • The hyena has Pounce: when it smells a bird and does not catch it, it smells again and may move more square. The badger burrows when it stays still, on any square. Moving brings it up.
  • After a knock-out the hunter moves 3 squares and does not pounce.
  • Landing on a bird catches it, and so does a bird landing on a hunter it could not see. A caught bird scrambles away in a random direction chosen by the game, and does not miss a turn. Only the bird's side knows where it went. 2 catches knock a bird out.

2 vs 2

  • Ostriches 1 and 2 team up. Each hides its own 2-egg nest; either ostrich can warm any egg.
  • Vultures 1 and 2 share every sniff. A stone is never thrown at your teammate's square.
  • Vultures on the same team see each other's found eggs.
  • Turns: Vulture 1, Ostrich 1, Vulture 2, Ostrich 2.
  • Vultures win when every egg is smashed or cold. Ostriches win if any egg is alive after round 30. A team stays in while one of its birds is.
  • A starved ostrich is out; its partner plays on. 10-second shot clock.

Hunters

  • One ostrich, one vulture, a hyena and a badger. Turns: vulture, ostrich, hyena, badger. 10-second shot clock.
  • The hyena has Pounce: when it smells a bird and does not catch it, it smells again and may move more square. The badger burrows when it stays still, on any square. Moving brings it up. 2 catches knock a bird out.
  • When one hunter moves onto the other, the mover pushes the other away and nobody is hurt. A hyena that walks onto a buried badger just shares its square, and the badger may burst out and shove it.
  • The hunters win when both birds are out: the hunter with more knock-outs wins; if equal, the one with more catches; if still equal, they tie.

2 vs 2 + hunter

  • Same as 2 vs 2, plus a hyena or badger that moves last.
  • Vultures on the same team see each other's found eggs.
  • The hyena has Pounce: when it smells a bird and does not catch it, it smells again and may move more square. The badger burrows when it stays still, on any square. Moving brings it up. 2 catches knock a bird out.
  • Hunter goal:
  • Knocked-out birds leave the board.
