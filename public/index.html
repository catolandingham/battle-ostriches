How to play

🐦 Ostrich

  • Hide eggs. Keep one alive for rounds.
  • Each turn: step 1 square, or sit on an egg.
  • 🔥 on an egg = turns until it goes cold. Sitting resets it.
  • Sit times in a row and you get hungry: eat the 🦗 first.
  • An egg you sit on is safe. A carried egg is not.
  • Carry an egg up to 2 turns, then set it down on a square no stone has hit.
  • 🪹 Fake nests fool the vulture.

🦅 Vulture

  • Smash all eggs to win.
  • Each turn: fly up to squares, or throw a stone next to you.
  • 👣 Darker footprints = more ostrich visits.
  • 👃 A glow when you land = an egg is close.
  • You hold 3 stones. Land on a 🪨 rock to reload.

Marks

  • 💥 smashed egg · ✕ missed · 🪶 egg was guarded · 🥚 egg you found · ● vulture landed (ostrich only)
