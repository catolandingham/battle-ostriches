How to play

🐦 Ostrich

  • Hide eggs. Keep one alive for rounds. Both birds are always visible; your eggs are not.
  • Each turn: Step 1 square, Sit on an egg (max in a row), or Carry egg 1 square.
  • The ring on each egg drains as it cools; the number is rounds left. Stand on it within rounds or it goes cold.
  • Step on a green leaf at least every turns or you starve.
  • A stone thrown at you just bounces off: your square (and an egg you carry) is always safe. Squares next to you are not. You can't carry an egg onto a gray stone mark. Smashed squares (red X) are blocked.

🦅 Vulture

  • Smash every egg (or outlast them: cold eggs and a starved ostrich count too).
  • Each turn: Fly up to squares, or Throw stone at your square or one next to it. A stone at the ostrich just bounces off (it protects its egg). A miss leaves a gray stone mark for the rest of the game; the ostrich won't carry an egg onto it. The vulture eats eggs and never needs food.
  • After each landing you learn Egg nearby! or No egg nearby (within square). This turn only.
  • You hold stone. Land on a gray rock pile to pick one up. Landing on an egg finds it (big yellow egg).

🐾 3 players

  • Turn order: vulture, ostrich, hunter. Each turn has a 10-second shot clock; if it runs out the turn ends (pass or stay put).
  • The hunter (hyena or honey badger) moves 1 square and only learns yes/no whether a bird is within 2 squares. North is the top of the board. Northwest, northeast, southwest, and southeast are the diagonals, and they count the same as north, south, east, and west. The sense does not point at the bird. Birds see the hunter only within 2 squares (never a burrowed badger). The honey badger burrows by staying still. It usually hides at once. Tough ground means stay still one more time. Moving pops it up.
  • Landing on a bird catches it (so does a bird stepping onto the hunter): it is knocked back 2 squares and loses its next turn. That bird is hurt until it is knocked out. Hyena: 2 catches knock a bird out. When its sense says yes, Pounce is one extra 1-square move on the same turn, and landing on the bird catches it. Honey badger: 1 catch; stay still to Burrow.
  • The hunter wins only by knocking out both birds before round 30; after the first knock-out it moves 2. A bird left alone still has to meet its own goal. A starved ostrich is out.

👥 4 players (2 vs 2)

  • Ostriches 1 and 2 team up. Each hides its own 2-egg nest; either ostrich can warm any egg.
  • Vultures 1 and 2 share every sniff.
  • Turns: Vulture 1, Ostrich 1, Vulture 2, Ostrich 2.
  • Vultures win when every egg is smashed or cold. Ostriches win if any egg is alive after round 30.
  • A starved ostrich is out; its partner plays on. 10-second shot clock.

🐾 4 players: Hunters

  • One ostrich, one vulture, a hyena and a honey badger. Turns: vulture, ostrich, hyena, badger. 10-second shot clock.
  • Hyena: Pounce; 2 catches knock a bird out. Honey badger: Burrow, usually on the first still turn (tough ground takes one more); 1 catch knocks a bird out. No speed boost after a knock-out.
  • Fearless rivals: if the badger moves onto the hyena, the hyena is knocked back 2. If the hyena moves onto the badger, the hyena bounces back 2. Nobody is hurt.
  • The hunters win when both birds are out: the hunter with more knock-outs wins (a tie goes to whoever got the last one). The birds win by their own goals.

🐾 5 players (2 vs 2 + hunter)

  • Same as 4 players, plus a hyena or honey badger that moves last. A honey badger burrows by staying still: usually at once, or one more still turn on tough ground.
  • The hunter wins by knocking out at least one ostrich AND one vulture. After its first knock-out it moves 2.
  • Knocked-out birds leave the board.

Marks

  • Glowing yellow square = you can move here · red-orange glow = throw here · gray stone = a stone landed here (the ostrich won't carry an egg onto it) · green leaf = food · gray stones = rock pile · big yellow egg = found egg · red X = smashed (blocked). Hover or tap a square to see what it is.
