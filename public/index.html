How to play

Blue Ostrich hides eggs. Red Vulture hunts them. Each player uses their own phone and sees only their side.

Ostrich (blue)

  • Each turn: Step one square (any of 8 directions), or Sit if you're on an egg.
  • Carry: while on an egg, step away taking it with you (one egg at a time). Set down takes a whole turn.
  • Warmth: every egg must be sat on at least once every rounds or it goes cold. Carrying doesn't warm it.
  • Feeding: after rounds in a row on the same egg you must step off.
  • The egg you're standing on (or carrying) can't be smashed. Spread eggs out to hide them — but then your warming rounds get longer.

Vulture (red)

  • Each turn: Fly up to squares (reveals nothing), or Throw a stone at your square or an adjacent one.
  • You see the ostrich's footprints everywhere — darker means crossed more often.
  • Black + orange burst = egg smashed. Dark X = miss. Feather = an egg was there but the ostrich guarded it. You may hit any square again (eggs move!).

Ostrich wins if any egg survives rounds. Vulture wins when all eggs are gone.
