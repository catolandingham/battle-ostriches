How to play

🐦 Ostrich

  • Hide eggs. Keep one alive for rounds.
  • Each turn: Step 1 square, Sit on an egg, or Carry egg 1 square.
  • 🔥 on an egg = rounds until it goes cold. Being on it resets it.
  • An egg under you is safe. Sit at most turns in a row, then step off for a turn.
  • A carried egg lands where you stand next turn. A stone on you before then smashes it.

🦅 Vulture

  • Smash every egg to win.
  • Each turn: Fly up to squares, or Throw stone at your square or one next to it. Stones never run out.
  • 👣 Footprints: bigger number = more ostrich visits.
  • 👃 Where you landed: bright = an egg within squares, faded = none.
  • Landing on an unguarded egg reveals it until it is moved.

Marks

  • 💥 smashed egg · ✕ stone hit nothing · 🪶 egg was guarded · ● vulture landed (ostrich only)
