How to play

Ostrich

Keep one of your 3 eggs alive for 30 rounds. Both birds always see each other; your eggs stay hidden.
Walk up to 2 squares. You may pass over anything, but not land on a wrecked square or on an animal you can see. Carry an egg 1 square, never onto or next to a leaf.
Sit on an egg as long as you like. A sitting ostrich is seen by anyone who can see it.
Kick an animal next to you instead of walking: it goes away 3 squares (the badger 2), around the edge of the board if needed. It stops early before a wrecked square or another animal. Not while carrying, and not a buried badger.
Stand or sit on each egg within 15 rounds or it goes cold. A carried egg is not warmed. The ring on an egg counts down.
Land on a leaf, or stay on one, every 15 turns or you starve.
A stone thrown at your square is kicked back at the vulture. Squares next to you are not safe.
Vulture

Break every egg. Cold eggs and a starved ostrich count too.
Fly up to 2 squares, or Throw at your square or one next to it. A stone wrecks the square it lands on, except a leaf or a rock pile, and pushes any animal there 2 squares away from you. Never at your teammate's square.
Each landing sniffs: Egg nearby! or No egg nearby (within 1 square). The result stays until you fly again.
You hold 1 stone. Land on rocks to get another. Landing on an egg finds it.
