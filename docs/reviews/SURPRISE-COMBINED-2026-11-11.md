# Surprise Combined pattern — 2026-11-11

Brian chose Combined for the kick and asked us to apply the same path carefully to the other surprise moments so he should not need to review each one.

Approved words used: "Look out!", "Tap to go on.", "You're on the egg!", and every already-approved event line. No new player-facing words.

## Timing

- Look out!: 1s, affected phone only, only after the action is committed.
- Cartoon: 2s, board frozen under it.
- Real motion: 0.6s per square. The landing piece and stage YOU stay hidden until arrival; YOU travels with the moving copy; wraps enter the opposite edge.
- Affected player then holds until they tap "Tap to go on." or an 8s cap.
- Why 8s: long enough to read the approved line and tap on a phone, shorter than one 10s shot clock, so a pocketed phone cannot freeze the table. The cap (and the tap) do not run anyone else's clock. If the affected player is next, their 10s starts only when they tap or the cap fires.
- Actors and bystanders are not blocked. They auto-continue after the cartoon and motion, and their status line is held at least 1s, then 0.3s per word (never a 2s flash).
- Bots: clock delay 0, so bot games cannot stall.
- Pass-and-play: each seat still uses its own previous view, so the incoming affected seat still gets the cartoon.

## Who gets the full treatment

- Kick (including wraps in 3p, 2v2, 5p, and Hunters): the kicked animal.
- Catch / pounce / flee, and knock-out: the caught bird (same 1s Look out for a hidden ambush and a catch in the open, so the beat is not a tell).
- Stone: each animal the stone actually hit.
- Egg smash: only the egg's owner (everyone is still told with the existing line).
- Badger burst, hunter shove: the animal that was shoved.

Actors and bystanders (including a 2v2 teammate) do not get Look out! or the tap hold.

## Look out skipped on purpose

These are not a sudden hit from someone else, and a 1s beat there could leak or confuse: dug-in, pop-out, sniff, found egg, starvation, a cold egg.

## Hidden information

The picture and the Look out beat follow the view the server already sends. A phone that is not told about the event gets no cartoon, no Look out, and no clock delay. Hidden trail squares stay redacted. Colors are never named.
